Projects

  • Ember (June-October 2026, paused) An attempt to train a foundation model from scratch on one RTX 4090, with no borrowed weights (paused). Not achieved: no checkpoint admitted, early-checkpoint benchmark scores at chance.
  • The Search (March-June 2026, concluded) More than 1,000 agent-run experiments on self-improving learners. Mostly negative: no learned action selection beat random actions; transfer was zero.
  • WEB-CAD (2026) Browser CAD with an in-browser Gemma 4 model. Its C++ kernel matched OpenCASCADE on 7 of 7 box/box boolean cases (the only cases tested; the NURBS check was skipped); the model runs at about 2 tokens/s.
  • All projects, including ember-cli, gemma-architect, Genesis Forge and a weight-space experiment.
