Ember

Can one person with one consumer graphics card train a language model from scratch, with no borrowed weights, teacher models or judge models, and keep only the training that passes a check?

June–October 2026. Code · Research log

The project, in its README's words: “build an AI model from scratch on one home computer with one consumer graphics card”, an RTX 4090-class 24 GB card. The target is a sparse decoder for text, images and audio with a minimum of three billion parameters; that is a goal, not a measurement. Only text (and some image-text) training has run.

Ember is the “learn only from verified experience” direction built and run: training happens in hour-long steps, and each hour's checkpoint is kept only if it passes a recorded check. The idea is close to existing work; no novelty is claimed.

What was built

The gated training loop. A rejected checkpoint is not kept and adds nothing to the chain; the rule is in code.

A research log in which every hypothesis must carry a pass/fail criterion written before the run, with an index of 1,022 run records.

ember-cli, the terminal tool used to run and watch training. A branch, not merged or released, adds a mode that runs third-party open-weights models on one GPU.

What was measured

Training kept across the chain: about 6.06 billion token positions over 1.48 million steps, 33 checkpoints deep, with a check that each hour is counted once.

Benchmarks were run only on an early checkpoint trained on 51,200 tokens, which is not a trained model, and were at chance: ARC-Challenge 0.2295 and HellaSwag 0.2588 (chance 0.25). No benchmark of the kept chain is published.

A measurement that was wrong

A profiling probe reported about 1.76 s per training step. Training actually ran with CUDA graph capture at about 0.19 s, and the probe had never called capture, so 76 of 142 records described a step that training never took. The probe was replaced by one that stops instead of reporting when capture did not happen.

A data decision

An audit found that the largest prose source, FineWeb-Edu (about 29% of corpus bytes), had been selected by a classifier trained on Llama-3-70B-Instruct annotations: an outside model's influence on the data. It was dropped. Whether the current corpus is clean is not established.

What is open

The main goal was not reached: “No current checkpoint is an Ember model”, and the latest candidate is not accepted.

Links pinned to commit df2a2d6.

More detail

Exact counts for the kept chain: 6,064,082,944 token positions over 1,480,489 steps, 33 checkpoints deep.

The early benchmarks covered 1,172 ARC-Challenge questions and 10,042 HellaSwag items. An older checkpoint from step 50 (819,200 tokens) scored 0.2227 on ARC-Challenge, also at chance.

ember-cli's open-weights mode, on one 24 GiB RTX 4090, selected a 27-billion-parameter model at 4-bit (a 16.4 GiB file) with an estimated 21.2 GiB of GPU memory, and a 131,072-token context by default. The model is a third-party open model, not an Ember-trained one. The guide's table for other cards is computed, not measured on each card. No speed measurement is published.
