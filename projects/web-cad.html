WEB-CAD

A CAD workbench that “runs entirely in the browser: no server, no API key, no install”, where a small language model turns a prompt into geometry you can keep editing.

April–June 2026. Live demo · Code

An experimental workbench, “not a production replacement for Rhino, Revit, or ArchiCAD”. It grew out of gemma-architect, an entry to the Gemma 4 Good Hackathon whose bundled demos are answered from a 60-row prompt cache, not generated live.

What was built

An in-browser agent using Gemma 4 E4B-it through Transformers.js and WebGPU; NURBS surfaces with IFC4 export; a C++ geometry kernel compiled to WebAssembly (booleans, fillet, chamfer, loft); and an MCP server that lets coding agents drive the geometry tools.

What was measured

The C++ kernel against OpenCASCADE: 7 of 7 box/box boolean cases matched in volume within 0.1%, and 60 of 60 robustness fuzz cases ran without a crash. Only box/box booleans were tested; the NURBS check was skipped.

In-browser decoding ran at a median 2.13 tokens per second (range 1.96–2.23, 25 prompts, WebGPU, on the gemma-architect deployment); the file does not record the hardware.

What is open

The README lists “AI agent accuracy varies” among its known limits, and the AI needs WebGPU. At about 2 tokens per second the live model is slow.

Links pinned to commit c672cb9.

More detail

gemma-architect, the hackathon entry WEB-CAD grew from, was built for the Gemma 4 Good Hackathon (Kaggle and Google DeepMind), deadline 2026-05-18. It ran Gemma 4 E4B-it, stock and not fine-tuned, with the replicad geometry kernel and web-ifc, in a single browser tab: the model writes a short geometry program, and the browser meshes it with OpenCASCADE and can export IFC4.

Its own tests: 9 of 9 bundled demos pass a self-test without a browser, and 19 of 19 rows of a small design-language corpus compile. Some rows of its prompt cache come from an earlier fine-tuned Gemma 3 model's outputs. Its write-up is marked a historical draft whose claims were not independently verified; IFC import, for example, was checked only by re-parsing, not in Revit or ArchiCAD.
