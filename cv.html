Research projects

  • Ember, June–October 2026. Training a language model from scratch on one RTX 4090, with no borrowed weights, teacher models or judge models; each hourly checkpoint kept only if it passed a recorded check. No checkpoint yet meets the project’s own bar. Includes ember-cli, its terminal tool.
  • The Search, March–June 2026. A falsification study of self-improving learners: more than 1,000 experiments across 14 architecture families. In the first era no learned action selection beat random actions; measured transfer was zero.
  • WEB-CAD, April–June 2026. A browser CAD workbench with an in-browser Gemma 4 model; grew out of the hackathon entry gemma-architect.
  • Genesis Forge, 2026. An offline Windows desktop app around Microsoft’s TRELLIS 3D model; most of the model code is Microsoft’s.
